Nutrition

Carbs aren't the enemy. And you don't have to give up the foods you love.

No foods off limits. Just ways to make the ones you love work for you. Not a diet, a lifestyle you can actually keep. Included in monthly coaching.

How it works

Eat without the guilt.

Most of my clients come to me saying the same thing: "I don't know what to do or eat." They've tried the apps, the cutting-out, the Monday restarts. Nothing stuck because it all asked them to live like someone else.

So we don't do that. You tell me the foods your family actually eats, and I show you how to make them work: a few swaps, a grocery list that fits one trip, macro targets you can hit without weighing everything. You get recipes and meal ideas, not a rulebook, and you can message me between sessions when the drive-through is winning.

The goal isn't a number. It's eating dinner with your kids without feeling like you ruined your progress, and having energy at 3pm.

What you get

  • Meal ideas and flexible guidelines
  • Recipes
  • Grocery lists
  • Macro or calorie targets
  • Messaging support between sessions

What I can't do

  • Write you a meal plan
  • Diagnose a condition
  • Coach a medical diet
For those, I'll refer you to a registered dietitian. Nursing an injury? See a physical therapist first.

Straight answers

Things moms ask about food.

Do I have to give up carbs?

No. Carbs aren't the enemy. We find ways to make the foods you already love work for you.

Do you write meal plans?

No, and I'll say so plainly: I'm not a dietitian. I give you meal ideas, recipes, grocery lists and targets, and I help you make your own food work.

Will I have to track everything?

You'll have targets, not a spreadsheet. Some moms like to log for a couple of weeks to learn portions; most don't need to after that.

I'm breastfeeding.

Tell her when you text. Targets change, and anything medical goes to your provider or a registered dietitian first.

Can I buy nutrition coaching on its own?

Right now it comes with the monthly training plan. Text Rachel if you only want the nutrition side and she'll tell you what she can do.
