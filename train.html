Before you book

Things moms ask about training.

I've never lifted weights.

Most of Rachel's clients hadn't. Your first session is just the two of you, and you'll learn four or five movements properly before anything gets heavy.

Where do sessions happen?

In person at a gym or partner location in Wilson, NC, in her garage gym, or on a video call. Say which you'd prefer when you text.

What should I bring?

Shoes you can move in, water, and the kids if you need to. That's it.

I'm postpartum. Is that okay?

Yes, and tell her when you text. If you're nursing an injury or need a medical diet, she'll refer you to a physical therapist or a dietitian first.

How do I pay?

Card, Stripe, Square, PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or Zelle. Self-pay only.
