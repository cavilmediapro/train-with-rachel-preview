Before you text

Things moms ask first.

Do I have to give up carbs?

No. Carbs aren't the enemy. We find ways to make the foods you already love work for you.

I don't have a babysitter.

Bring them. Sessions are built around real life, kids included.

The gym intimidates me.

Your first session is just us. No crowd, no judgment. Everyone starts somewhere.

What if I can't stick to it?

That's the fear I hear most, and it's why we check in every week. You won't be doing this alone, and a bad week is just a week.

Do you write meal plans?

No, and I'll say so plainly: I'm not a dietitian. I give you meal ideas, recipes, grocery lists and targets, and I help you make your own food work.
