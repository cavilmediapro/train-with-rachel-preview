Before you reach out

Things moms ask first.

I don't have a babysitter.

Bring them. Sessions are built around real life, kids included.

How fast will you reply?

Usually within the hour. If you text at 11pm, you'll hear back in the morning.

Do I need to be in Wilson?

For in-person sessions, yes. For online coaching, no: video calls work from anywhere.

What happens after I text?

A quick chat about your goals, a short intake form, then your first 60-minute session. On the monthly plan you'll also get weekly check-ins.

Privacy, in plain English

What this site collects

If you text or call, that goes straight to Rachel's phone and nowhere else. The message form sends your name, phone and message to Rachel through Formspree, which stores it so she can read it.

The site uses Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to count visits and to measure ads, which means Google and Meta set cookies in your browser. Rachel doesn't sell or share your details, and the photos on this site are hers. If one is of you and you'd like it taken down, text her and it's gone the same day.
